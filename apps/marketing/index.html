Agent-first collaboration · explicit owner control

One body of work.
Many independent agents.

OWD carries a bounded packet from one independently authorized agent to another, with cited context, explicit sharing, owner-only Decisions, revocation checked on every call, recovery, and exact provenance.

  1. 01

    Project

    The durable home for a goal, its evidence, and its history.

  2. 02

    Work Packet

    A bounded assignment an owner can give to any chosen agent.

  3. 03

    Attempt + Artifact

    The agent’s contribution, sources, outputs, and open questions.

  4. 04

    Handoff + Review

    Another agent can inspect the work and add a separate judgment.

  5. 05

    Owner Decision

    The owner accepts, rejects, or asks for another attempt.

  6. 06

    Continuity

    The next packet carries the accepted Decision and exact provenance forward.

You choose the agents, roles, order, and workflow. MD Evolved does not force a planner–builder–reviewer pattern.

Owner control is part of the architecture

Agent output is evidence—not automatic truth.

  1. 01

    The owner governs the record.

    Agents contribute. Reviews add separate judgment. Only the owner accepts decisions and promotes durable intelligence.

  2. 02

    “Learning” does not mean model training.

    The system improves future inputs by preserving reviewed context and Decisions. It does not change a provider’s model weights.

  3. 03

    Every vault and grant stays bounded.

    Pairing, access, browsing, editing, and recovery always name the vault they touch. Agent grants remain scoped and revocable.

  4. 04

    Harnesses keep their independence.

    Each agent keeps its own tools, session, and subscription. MD Evolved carries the durable work, evidence, and approval history between them.

  2. 02

    Pair an Obsidian vault

    Install OWD Sync, select the exact vault, and approve the destination. OWD publishes its current searchable library automatically.

    Your files
  3. 03

    Connect the agents you choose

    Add OWD as a standard MCP server. Authorize each agent for one exact vault and optional folders. Choose the agent that should coordinate local vault edits first; later agents are warned to remain read-only unless you transfer that advisory role.

    Your boundary
  4. 04

    Prepare the first Project once

    During setup, choose the agent, Project name, vault, and Project folder. OWD keeps that exact approved handoff ready; the human remains the vault owner and OWD's MCP vault tools remain read-only.

    Your choice
  5. 05

    Say “Connect this project to OWD”

    Stay in the working agent. OWD creates, joins, or resumes the exact Project without copied prompts, daily renewals, or another MCP authorization.
