Agent-first collaboration · explicit owner control

One body of work.
Many independent agents.

MDevolved carries a bounded packet from one independently authorized agent to another, with cited context, explicit sharing, owner-only Decisions, revocation checked on every call, recovery, and exact provenance.

  1. 01

    Project

    The durable home for a goal, its evidence, and its history.

  2. 02

    Work Packet

    A bounded assignment an owner can give to any chosen agent.

  3. 03

    Attempt + Artifact

    The agent’s contribution, sources, outputs, and open questions.

  4. 04

    Handoff + Review

    Another agent can inspect the work and add a separate judgment.

  5. 05

    Owner Decision

    The owner accepts, rejects, or asks for another attempt.

  6. 06

    Continuity

    The next packet carries the accepted Decision and exact provenance forward.

You choose the agents, roles, order, and workflow. MDevolved does not force a planner–builder–reviewer pattern.

Owner control is part of the architecture

Agent output is evidence—not automatic truth.

  1. 01

    The owner governs the record.

    Agents contribute. Reviews add separate judgment. Only the owner accepts decisions and promotes durable intelligence.

  2. 02

    “Learning” does not mean model training.

    The system improves future inputs by preserving reviewed context and Decisions. It does not change a provider’s model weights.

  3. 03

    Every Source and grant stays bounded.

    Pairing, access, browsing, editing, and recovery always name the Source they touch. Agent grants remain scoped and revocable.

  4. 04

    Harnesses keep their independence.

    Each agent keeps its own tools, session, and subscription. MDevolved carries the durable work, evidence, and approval history between them.

Guided once · agent-first after that

Set up MDevolved once.
Then work from the agent.

The MDevolved workspace walks you through a Source, searchable library, agents, and first Project. After that, Project work begins in the agent you already use.

  1. 01

    Open your MDevolved workspace

    A managed tester opens one private invitation to one isolated workspace. The Community edition will deploy the same owner-controlled data plane to your Cloudflare account.

    Your workspace
  2. 02

    Choose a Markdown folder or Obsidian

    Open MDevolved Sync and choose a Markdown folder, or install its compatibility-preserving Obsidian adapter. Approve the exact Source; MDevolved publishes its current searchable library automatically.

    Your files
  3. 03

    Connect the agents you choose

    Add MDevolved as a standard MCP server. Authorize each agent for one exact Source and optional folders. Choose the agent that should coordinate local edits first; later agents are warned to remain read-only. A restarted process can resume through that same authorized client; a different authorization stays read-only.

    Your boundary
  4. 04

    Prepare the first Project once

    During setup, choose the agent, Project name, Source, and Project folder. MDevolved keeps that exact approved handoff ready; the human remains the Source owner and its MCP compatibility tools remain read-only.

    Your choice
  5. 05

    Say “Connect this project to MDevolved”

    Stay in the working agent. MDevolved creates, joins, or resumes the exact Project without copied prompts, daily renewals, or another MCP authorization.

    Agent-first
  6. 06

    Keep what should survive

    Carry cited Artifacts, Reviews, owner Decisions, and approved context forward—with revocation and encrypted recovery still under owner control.

    Your record
